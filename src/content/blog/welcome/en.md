Hello, trainers!

We have launched the official Evolor website. Here you will find everything about the game: its world, monsters and battles, as well as news about updates and events.

## What's on the site

- **The Game** — an overview of the main mechanics: catching, evolution, battles, PvP, quests and outfits.
- **News** — this blog. Patch notes, events and development notes will appear here.
- **Company** — a few words about our studio and how to contact us.

## What's next

We are working on new regions, monsters and seasonal events. Follow the news here and join our Discord to share your ideas — many features of the game started there.

See you in the game!
